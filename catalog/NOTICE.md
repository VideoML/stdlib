# Hyperframes inventory provenance and notices

The inventory references [Hyperframes](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) at commit `28181602fe2d9ffa38474a6e8155a89909a7b8aa`. The upstream repository declares Apache License 2.0 in its root `LICENSE` (a copy is retained at `licenses/Apache-2.0.txt`); individual registry items sometimes declare additional licenses, which are retained verbatim in the manifest. Each entry also lists the relevant notice and license files found beneath its source directory. The source paths are references to that pinned revision; this inventory does not copy those source files or their media.

License entries describe source declarations, not approval to redistribute every embedded or remotely referenced asset. Third-party dependencies, fonts, images, audio, and other media remain unreviewed. An implementation packet must inspect its cited notices and source assets, retain required attribution and license texts, resolve dependency versions, and use an appropriately licensed neutral replacement where rights are unknown. The empty local fixture lists do not represent reused upstream assets.

The 456 registry entries map one-to-one to the 92 C packets in `packet-map.json`; the 13 shader registry entries are assigned to P08. All 469 entries are planned. No catalog entry is implemented or verified by this inventory.
